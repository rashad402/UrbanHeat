How ΔT is estimated. This preview linearises the surface energy balance around each ward's measured temperature: raising albedo cuts absorbed shortwave, and adding vegetation adds latent (evaporative) cooling, each scaled by the ward's built-up share and the restoring term 4εσT³ + ρcp/rah. Effects are clamped to published tropical field ranges (cool roofs ≈1.5–4 °C, greening ≈0.5–3 °C). It is a physically-grounded stand-in, not the trained PINN — the PINN will replace this estimator once trained, using the same ward inputs. Baseline LST is the 2015–2026 dry-season Landsat mean; 27 water-dominated wards use the city-mean baseline (shown fainter).
